Pet Hair Removal

Dog hair weaves itself into carpet and cloth, and a vacuum alone won't pull it out. We remove it from cargo areas, seats and floors.

Why it matters: Embedded hair holds dander and odor, and it's one of the first things a buyer or dealer notices at trade-in.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Do you come to me?

Yes. LED Detailing is mobile, so we can detail your car at your home or work anywhere in Cincinnati or Northern Kentucky. You can also bring it to our location.

What areas do you serve?

Cincinnati, Ohio and Northern Kentucky. If you're not sure whether you're in range, call or text (513) 615-9437.

Can you really get dog hair out?

Yes. Pet hair removal is one of our core services. See the before-and-after sliders on our work page for real examples.

What is extraction?

Extraction deep-cleans carpet, mats and cloth seats by working a cleaning solution into the fibers and pulling it back out along with the dirt, stains and odor trapped inside.

How much does a detail cost?

It depends on the size and condition of the vehicle, so every job is quoted. Send your year, make and model (photos help) through the form, by text or on Instagram and we'll get you a price.

How do I book?
